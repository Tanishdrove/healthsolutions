About Us

Specialty Diagnostics
with Compassion

P3 Health Solutions LLP is a state-of-the-art diagnostic laboratory committed to delivering accurate, reliable, and timely test results. We combine advanced analytical technology with a patient-first approach, making quality healthcare accessible to everyone.

Our fully automated laboratory is staffed by qualified pathologists, microbiologists, and technicians who uphold the highest standards at every step — from sample collection to report delivery.

  • Fully automated, NABL-certified laboratory
  • Home sample collection available 7 days a week
  • Digital reports delivered via WhatsApp & email
  • Expert team of pathologists and specialists

Our Services

Comprehensive Diagnostic Services

From routine blood work to advanced specialty panels — we cover it all with precision and care.

Haematology

Complete blood count, ESR, peripheral smear, coagulation profiles and more.

Biochemistry

Liver function, kidney function, lipid profiles, glucose, thyroid, and electrolytes.

Microbiology

Culture & sensitivity, urine culture, blood culture, stool examination, and AFB.

Immunology & Serology

HIV, HBsAg, HCV, Widal, VDRL, dengue, typhoid, and autoimmune panels.

Urine & Stool Analysis

Routine urine examination, 24-hr urine tests, occult blood, and stool microscopy.
